Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how THE LAST CLUE (“the game”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit or play the browser-based detective game at the-last-clue-six.vercel.app. The game is an independently developed project.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, the game may process:

  • Account information: information you provide when registering or signing in, such as an email address and account identifier.
  • Gameplay and session data: case/session identifiers, discovered evidence, investigation actions, answers, scores, and session status needed to run a case and calculate results.
  • Information stored in your browser: preferences such as theme, case progress, notes, and other investigation state may be stored in local storage on your device.
  • Technical and usage information: the hosting, security, and analytics services used by the site may process technical information such as browser/device details, network identifiers, and page interactions as described in their own policies.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into investigation notes or other game fields.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide account access, create and restore game sessions, save or display progress, calculate case results, maintain security, diagnose errors, understand site usage, and operate or improve the game.

4. Browser storage and cookies

THE LAST CLUE uses browser local storage for certain preferences and gameplay state. Hosting, analytics, authentication, or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their services. You can manage browser storage and cookies through your browser settings; clearing them may remove locally saved game progress or preferences.

5. Analytics and hosting providers

The site is hosted using Vercel and includes Vercel Analytics. These services may process technical or usage data to deliver, protect, and measure the service. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy documentation: Vercel Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

We intend to use Google AdSense to display advertisements if the site is approved and ads are enabled. When advertising is active, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google policies. Learn more in Google's advertising information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising is not guaranteed to be active at the time you read this policy. Where required by law, applicable consent and privacy choices should be provided before relevant advertising technologies are used.

7. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, analytics, authentication, game functionality, security, or advertising. We do not describe those providers as acting independently of their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product.

8. Retention and your choices

Information stored in your browser remains there until you clear it or the browser removes it. Information handled by the game's server or service providers may be retained for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes, subject to the applicable provider's practices. You can stop using the game and clear local site data through your browser settings.

9. Children's privacy

THE LAST CLUE is a general-audience mystery game and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the project page so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the game's features, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means the updated policy is available for review; it does not override rights or consent requirements provided by law.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy-related requests, contact the developer through the THE LAST CLUE GitHub repository.
